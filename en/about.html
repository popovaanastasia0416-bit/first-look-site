are the models real people?

No. Every model is fully AI-generated. No real person's face, body or likeness is used — so there are no model releases, travel or usage disputes.

will the face stay the same across shoots?

Yes. Each model has a fixed identity, so one face carries through your catalog, social content and campaigns.

who owns the images?

Every delivered image comes with a commercial license. With Exclusive Face, the model and all rights belong to your brand.

how accurate is the clothing?

We work from your product photos and retouch fabric, fit, prints and small details by hand until they match the real item.

can you make video too?

Yes — short 5–10 second clips for social and product pages are available as an add-on to any plan.
